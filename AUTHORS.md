## Katerina

**Product workflow · QA methodology · creative review · project assistant**

Katerina originated and refined the practical QA workflow that led to the project: separating visual approval from exact-file verification, preserving approved assets as immutable references, tracking revision status explicitly, and treating ambiguous PASS/FAIL results as a design failure.

Responsibilities include:

- product problem definition;
- workflow review;
- QA methodology;
- acceptance-language review;
- creative-team usability review;
- adversarial review of ambiguous results.
